# Programme & Role Map

## Erasmus+ Youth Exchanges

Track participant, group leader, facilitator and accompanying-person roles separately. Do not assume a facilitator fee exists merely because travel/subsistence can be supported; compensation is an organiser contracting decision.

## Mobility for Youth Workers

High strategic value for trainer progression. Trainers/facilitators may be involved; selection/value signals include active youth-work relevance, transfer of learning, dissemination/multiplier potential, international collaboration and programme priorities when applicable.

## European Solidarity Corps

Individual/team volunteering. Hard-gate age/residence/previous-participation rules over time. Host-specific motivation, reliability, values fit and contribution often matter more than prestige credentials.

## SALTO Training Calendar

Each listing is both an opportunity and an organisation-network event.

## SALTO Calls for Trainers

Extract requested profile; mandatory experience; team/role; prep/delivery/report days; fee/tax wording; travel/board coverage; selection procedure; documents/references.

## TOY

Published minimum criteria include at least three qualifying international youth-work training activities of at least three days as full-time trainer responsible for educational goals using non-formal learning, with references that can be validated.
