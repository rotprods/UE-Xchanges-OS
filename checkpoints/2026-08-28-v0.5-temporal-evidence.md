## Temporal invariant

Separate:

- historical youth-sector experience;
- current youth-work involvement;
- delivery/facilitation responsibility;
- trainer qualification.

The new regression requires:

`historical_experience=true + 2 Youth Staff + 1 Youth Exchange + current_context=null + call_requires_current_context => eligibility UNKNOWN`.

Historical participation may improve programme-literacy/fit/organisation priors. It never bypasses a current-context hard gate.
