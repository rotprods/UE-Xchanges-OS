## Stop contract

### Primary stop — PASS

All conditions hold:

- EYP/ESC account and PRN are live privately;
- every T0/T1 row is `SUBMITTED_WITH_RECEIPT`, `TERMINAL_OBJECTIVE`, or `WAITING_EXTERNAL_EVIDENCE` with a sent query, owner and dated follow-up;
- Building With Our Hands and Behind the Scenes are resolved;
- at least 2 receipts are stored, target 5;
- Telegram source backlog is 60/60 resolved/inaccessible/invalid with provenance;
- no deadline inside 72 hours is unowned;
- state projections agree and final QA passes.

### Conditional stop — evidence-based

Allowed only if fewer than two valid submissions were objectively possible. Requires a per-call blocker report proving every candidate was terminal or externally blocked and that no route was abandoned for low fit, inconvenience, duration or option cost.

### No-stop conditions

- open deadline inside 72 hours without one next action;
- submission without receipt evidence;
- wrong-country or unauthorised form;
- unresolved duplicate risk;
- final AI-authored prose under `AI_UNKNOWN` or prohibited policy;
- source backlog that may hide current deadlines;
- material Drive/GitHub/Todoist divergence.
