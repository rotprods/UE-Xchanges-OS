# Global Mobility and Income Strategy

Effective: 2026-08-29

## Portfolio objective

Enable Roberto to expand globally beyond Murcia while continuing or delegating online work. Execute every objectively viable route, but order effort toward the opportunities with the strongest verified economics and professional trajectory.

## Two simultaneous lanes

### Economic / professional

Prioritise legitimate paid work as:

1. trainer;
2. facilitator;
3. paid monitor, camp counsellor or activity leader;
4. project coordinator;
5. paid media, communication or digital specialist;
6. consultant or expert.

### Funded global experience

Continue applying to non-salary opportunities when travel, accommodation, meals or training are funded and the route offers exceptional experience, a rare destination, a valuable network or a credible trainer/facilitator credential step.

Neither lane excludes the other. `APPLY EVERYTHING VIABLE` remains active.

## Economics model

Primary:

```text
Verified net cash / verified work hour
```

Secondary:

```text
Verified net cash / committed calendar hour
```

Tertiary:

```text
Total verified net cash
```

Net cash excludes mandatory programme fees, unreimbursed travel, visa/insurance, estimated taxes or contract costs and other compulsory out-of-pocket costs.

Travel reimbursement, accommodation, meals, insurance, training and local transport are stored as non-cash funded value. They never become salary in the model.

When work hours or compulsory costs are unknown, hourly/net metrics remain `UNKNOWN` and generate economics verification debt.

## Scheduling score

After hard gates, the execution priority vector is:

| Dimension | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Paid cash-rate potential | 35% |
| Payment/contract certainty | 15% |
| Total net cash | 10% |
| Trainer/facilitator trajectory | 12% |
| Outside-Europe/globality | 10% |
| Rarity/scarcity | 8% |
| Remote-work compatibility | 5% |
| Experience/network value | 5% |

This score schedules work. It never changes eligibility, AI policy, safety or truth.

## Rarity

Rarity should be estimated from evidence:

- frequency of comparable calls in the source registry;
- scarcity of the role;
- destination frequency;
- access to unusual institutions, territories or communities;
- number of equivalent live alternatives.

A destination merely appearing attractive is not enough to assign a high rarity score.

## Geographic policy

Outside-Europe, neighbouring-partner-country, humanitarian, island, overseas-territory and remote-region opportunities receive strategic priority. There is no blanket country exclusion, but every application remains subject to current visa, safety, insurance, health, tax, legal, internet and host-condition checks.

## Remote-work compatibility

Long and paid placements require explicit evidence for:

- scheduled duty hours and days;
- time-zone overlap;
- minimum connectivity;
- accommodation/workspace conditions;
- employer/host restrictions;
- tax and visa implications;
- protected weekly hours for Roberto's businesses;
- delegation feasibility.

“Can work online” is not sufficient by itself to pass a full-time commitment gate.

```text
→ L1 verified affiliation/collaboration

```

The current route is to convert verified AI/media/communication expertise into a real youth-facing workshop, then co-facilitation, references and paid calls. Historical participation supports programme literacy but never proves trainer responsibility.

## Measurable coverage

Literal proof of “every vacancy on the Internet” is impossible. The operational definition of complete coverage is:

- every active registered source scanned inside SLA;
- every returned item preserved with provenance;
- deterministic dedupe to one canonical opportunity;
- every external link resolved toward an original source/form/infopack;
- every unresolved, inaccessible or stale frontier counted;
- every viable call routed to submission or an objective block.

Metrics:

```text
Source Freshness Coverage
Discovery Resolution Coverage
Canonical Application Coverage
Paid Economics Coverage
```

## Submission policy

For every opportunity:

```text
source → identity → deadline/Spain/role gates → infopack → exact form
→ AI policy → evidence map → answer pack → human-owned final assets
→ authorised submission → durable receipt
```

Infopacks are analysed. Forms and attached templates are completed using verified real information. Arbitrary authenticated portals and CAPTCHA require the applicant's legitimate final interaction unless a connected provider action explicitly supports submission.
