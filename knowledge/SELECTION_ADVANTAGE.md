# Selection Advantage Engine

## Principle

Win by reducing selector uncertainty, not embellishing the applicant.

A strong application answers: Why this person for this project? What will they contribute? What will they learn? Are they reliable? What remains after the mobility?

## Matrix

`criterion -> proof -> contribution -> learning goal -> multiplier output`

No proof = explicit gap, never fabrication.

## Differentiators

1. Exact project specificity.
2. Relevant contribution assets: facilitation support, documentation, creative/media production, digital tools, outreach, workshop co-design, dissemination.
3. Reliability: full dates, complete form, clear logistics, prompt replies.
4. Concrete multiplier effect after the mobility.
5. Evidence density: examples > adjectives.
6. Positive organiser relationships/references.
7. Role-aware positioning: participant applications should not read like an attempt to hijack the programme as trainer; trainer calls require educational responsibility/methods/outcomes.

## Penalties

Generic motivation -10..-25; project-name/copy mismatch blocks readiness; unsupported claim blocks the claim; contradiction forces review; AI-final-text prohibition disables draft generation; excessive self-promotion without contribution/learning balance -10; missing mandatory field/document => readiness 0.
